ECCA
Electroconvulsive Therapy Cognitive Assessment · bedside calculator

1. Orientation / 4

0 / 4

“Tell me today’s date.” (clarify: “Tell me the month and the year.”) · “Tell me the name of this building or place that we are in.”

2. Subjective Memory / 4

0 / 4

“I am going to ask you about how you feel your memory is right now. Please answer yes or no.” · 1 point per No.

3. Informant Assessment / 4

0 / 4

To informant: “I am going to ask your opinion about the patient’s memory. Please answer yes or no to the best of your knowledge.” · 1 point per No.

4. Registration / 0

“I am going to read a list of four words. Repeat them after me and try to memorize them. I will ask you about them at the end of the test.”

5. Attention / 3

0 / 3
Serial 7s — start at
Target sequence (5 subtractions)
Trail B —
Alternate numbers and letters. Any mistake disqualifies the point.

6. Autobiographical Memory / 6

0 / 6

Ask each item; judge against what the informant reported. 1 point per corroborated response.

7. Fund of Knowledge / 5

0 / 5

“I am going to ask general knowledge questions. Please answer to the best of your knowledge.”

8. Delayed Recall / 4

0 / 4

“I gave you four words earlier. Could you please tell me the four words?” · Tap each word recalled.
